Portfolio update notes
======================

This revision keeps the existing portfolio and updates only the Mammad interaction area.

Mammad interaction
------------------
- The hero now shows only a small clickable robot icon. Its tooltip says: «من ربات ممدم؛ برو روشنم کن».
- The actual robot is at the bottom of the page and is explicitly the interaction target.
- Touch/click the robot's head to soothe it; touch/click its torso to trigger a hurt reaction. Repeated hits trigger stronger, personality-specific responses.
- Visitors can switch among 12 robot personalities. The personality changes the robot's short reactions.
- There are separate random lines, multiple-choice questions about Mohammad, written questions about Mohammad, and a direct message path.
- Multiple-choice answers advance automatically. Written answers advance after the visitor submits each answer.
- Survey responses are collected into an ordered email payload for Mohammad.
- A visitor nickname can be saved in localStorage on the same browser/device. It is not synced across devices or browsers and can be cleared by the visitor's browser settings.

Email delivery
--------------
- The form uses FormSubmit and the contact address already used on the portfolio.
- The recipient may need to activate the form by following FormSubmit's first-use email confirmation. Delivery has not been verified from this local file; test it after publishing.
- A successful local button click alone does not prove that an email was delivered.

Private visitor analytics
-------------------------
- Analytics are not active in this archive. No analytics provider account or site ID was supplied.
- To count daily visitors privately, connect a provider such as Umami Cloud and keep its dashboard login private. GitHub Pages cannot securely hide an admin dashboard embedded in public HTML.
- Instagram source traffic can be measured with a dedicated UTM-tagged bio link, but website analytics cannot identify visitors' Instagram usernames just because they came from Instagram.
